Hi! I am a first-year physics Ph.D. student at Harvard University. I recently graduated from the University of California, Berkeley with majors in physics, computer science, and applied mathematics.

As an undergraduate, I performed research on quantum sensing with Prof. [Joel Moore](https://physics.berkeley.edu/people/faculty/joel-moore) and pattern formation with Prof. [Edgar Knobloch](https://physics.berkeley.edu/people/faculty/edgar-knobloch). I also studied outer planet atmospheres with Dr. [Mike Wong](https://research.ssl.berkeley.edu/~mikewong/bio.php) and two-dimensional materials with Prof. [Tongcang Li](https://sites.google.com/site/litongcang/) (Purdue University).

My CV is available [here](/files/troy_tsubota_CV.pdf).
